Jabaitedd Analytics: Terms of Service

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Jabaitedd Analytics is a private, internal tool for reading statistics of TikTok accounts operated by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else may sign in to it.

The tool only reads data through TikTok's official API with the account holder's permission. It never posts, comments, follows, or changes anything on TikTok. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service.

The tool is provided as is, without warranty. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
