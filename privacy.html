Jabaitedd Analytics: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Jabaitedd Analytics is a private, internal tool. It is used only by its owner to read performance statistics for TikTok accounts the owner operates. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

With the account holder's permission through Login with TikTok, it reads basic profile information (open ID, username, display name), account statistics (follower, like and video counts) and the public videos' statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

How it is used

The data is stored on the owner's own computer to track how the owner's content performs. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The tool never posts, comments, or changes anything on TikTok.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are kept in a private file on the owner's computer. Revoking the app in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) stops all access. Stored statistics can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/jabaitedd/media/issues.
